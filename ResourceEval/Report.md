The suggested benchmark to assign a value to each MWh produced is the capture price of that renewable.
$$Capture Price=\frac{\sum_{i=1}^n (electricity sold (MWh) \times market price (MWh))} {\sum_{i=1}^n Electricity sold (MWh)}$$\
Capture prices represent the average price per megawatt-hour (MWh) received by a renewable energy producer for electricity sold in the market. Unlike fixed electricity tariffs or Power Purchase Agreements (PPAs), capture prices fluctuate based on real-time market conditions, meaning that renewable energy generators may earn different amounts for each unit of electricity they produce. ([Enlitia.com](https://www.enlitia.com/resources-blog-post/capture-price-definition))

we can see that a capture price benchmark could give a more honest overview of a power plant's energy generation and its possible value for the market.
